Jacob's words throughout; only ordering, sectioning, and light trims are mine.
("Stitch" lowercased to "stitch" — assumed voice-text caps.)

Arrangement notes

The v2 lyrics grew a second thread: alongside the waiting ("tell me when to start")
there's now defiance ("I don't care," "test me if you dare," "wind back in my sails").
The arrangement lets that thread grow verse by verse instead of staying buried.

- The "I don't care / if you must" refrains become pressure valves: pre-chorus, then bridge.
- Verse 3 is a stripped breakdown — the opening verse with the waiting lines thinned out,
  so the song gets quiet before the peak.
- The final chorus is the defiant stanza, not a repeat of the waiting chorus.
  The song earns its peak instead of circling back.
- Outro tightened: kept the loop's spine, cut the middle cycle that restated verse
  material ("tell me where to start / but in the meantime..." round) and the second
  "all week / I don't care" reprise. The loop still lands where it always landed.
