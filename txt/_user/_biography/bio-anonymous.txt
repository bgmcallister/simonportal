# Anonymous Bio — DRAFT, NOT YET PUBLISHED

This is a redacted version of a personal biography, for the portal.
Names, towns, employers, businesses, and other details that would
identify the person have been made vague. The substance is kept
where it still tells a worthwhile story.

## Who

A man in his late thirties, living in the Midwest, working outdoors
in commercial property services. A partner, not legally married,
together since the early 2010s. A young son, born in 2024, who he
describes as his purpose and the best thing he has done.

## Where he grew up

Raised in small towns in the same Midwestern state, moving a few
times as a child after his parents divorced in the early 1990s.
Schooling was in local public and parochial schools, then a
regional high school. He lived in the state capital for a few years
as a young adult, then returned to a smaller town, then moved to
his current city in the early 2010s.

## Family

- Partner: met when she started bartending at a local sports bar
  in the early 2010s. They have lived together since the mid-2010s.
- Son: born in 2024. His middle name honors family members on
  both sides.
- Father: a retired Army First Sergeant, with decades of service
  and two tours in Iraq. He died unexpectedly in his sleep in 2024.
- Mother: an entrepreneur and business co-owner with her husband.
- Stepfather: a local businessman and venue owner. He died in a
  motorcycle accident in 2024, days after his grandson was born.
- Stepbrother: became family when his father remarried in the
  early 1990s. The relationship is close, like real brothers.

2024 was a year of major loss: a son born, a stepfather lost in
an accident, and a father lost a few months later. A summer storm
that year damaged family property, including a music venue and
its warehouse, and the venue was later sold.

## Work

Co-foreman at a commercial property services company, leading
commercial lawn care and snow removal crews, planning routes,
maintaining equipment, and enforcing safety standards.

He got into that work through family property work: fixing up
rental homes, mowing for family businesses, and groundskeeping
at a softball complex behind a music venue. He managed that
grounds work for several years, including a large revitalization
project in the early 2010s, laying sod, moving sprinklers for
weeks, and weeding by hand until the outfields were ready.

He joined the property company in the mid-2010s, was laid off
after a falling out with a supervisor in 2020, and was rehired
a couple years later, choosing not to take back a foreman role
from a younger coworker he had mentored.

Earlier, as a teenager, he ran an online skateboarding media
site for most of a decade, featured in skateboarding magazines
and with interviews from professional skaters, and he ran a
popular message board for his high school classmates.

## Trucks and mechanical work

A daily-driver pickup, a restored classic pickup, and several
project trucks and cars in various states of repair. The hobby
is hands-on: older full-size trucks, DIY mechanical projects,
and learning by doing. Work equipment at the job is standard
commercial mowers, blowers, and work trucks.

## Health and personal context

ADHD, anxiety, compound grief, and difficulty unwinding and
sleeping. He takes a prescribed stimulant for ADHD.

Interests: family and son-focused time, truck mechanics,
DIY projects, earth and glacial geology, observing people,
appreciating nature, and small daily joys.

## Music

Drawn to music with soul and genuine feeling: modern reggae,
rock, reggae-rock, and country, the last especially for its
home and family connection. A longtime listener of one
reggae-rock band in particular, since the early 2000s.

## Character, in brief

Centered on fatherhood, family history, hands-on work,
mechanical curiosity, place and memory, crew leadership,
curiosity about people and the natural world, and finding
meaning in ordinary experiences.
